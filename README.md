# Engineering Intelligence

An AI-assisted engineering intelligence system designed to help software engineers stay informed while preserving human judgment.

The goal is not to automate engineering decisions.

The goal is to reduce information overload by collecting, organizing and presenting evidence that helps engineers think critically about technology, architecture and industry changes.

## Principles

- AI gathers information.
- AI extracts evidence.
- AI explains its reasoning.
- Humans make engineering decisions.
- Transparency over magic.
